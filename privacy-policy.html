Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

HabitBase ("the app") is built and maintained by Chetan Rajesh. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

No Data Collection

HabitBase does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. All habit data you create is stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

iCloud Backups

If you choose to enable iCloud backups, your habit data is saved to your personal iCloud account. This data is controlled entirely by you and is never accessed by Chetan Rajesh or any third party.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

NFC

If you use the NFC feature to link physical tags to habits, all NFC data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

HabitBase does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

HabitBase does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
